ARCH ACRE is a principal-led architecture and design consultancy. We plan, draw and deliver buildings and public places across Pakistan — from the first site visit to the final handover.

We design and deliver architecture, interiors and public places — drawn in-house, detailed to build, and built with care.

Founded in Lahore and registered with PCATP in 2023, the studio works across architecture, interior design, town planning, urban planning, project management and landscaping.

What we do

01

Architecture

Concept to construction drawings — homes, façades, monuments and public buildings, detailed for real sites and real budgets.

 02

Interior

Layouts, materials and joinery drawn for local craftsmen — from a single room to a complete residence.

 03

Town planning

Layout plans, plot rationalisation and authority submissions prepared to the standards of the approving body.

 04

Urban planning

Master plans, street design and public realm strategies for markets, gateways and civic fronts.

 05

Project management

BOQs, tendering, site supervision and handover — one point of responsibility from drawing to completion.

 06

Landscaping

Hard and soft landscape, water features and street furniture, coordinated with the architecture.

 07

Visualization

Rendered views show how a design will look and help clients and contractors understand it before construction.
